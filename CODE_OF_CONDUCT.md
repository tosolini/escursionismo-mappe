# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful and inclusive
- Accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Trolling, personal attacks, or public/private intimidation
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

## Reporting

Report incidents to: conduct@tosolini.info
